In my first semester of grade 10, I took Computer Studies, where we learned to code in Scratch. The class was structured to give a lot of time, around a month, to our final project, which we would present in a classwide show.

I decided to build a 3D game in Scratch, which proved to be incredibly difficult given my skills and knowledge at the time, not to mention the platform. 

I wanted to build the whole thing myself, meaning no documentation or research of any kind. I did not manage to invent the view frustum, and instead pulled from previous knowledge on the inverse-square law to try and mimic light propagation. The thought process here is nonsensical, but it created a cool horizon effect by essentially applying the inverse square law twice, once implicitly and then again explicitly.
